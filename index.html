03 / SKIN HEALTH GUIDE

Remember the
ABCDE rule.

These are warning signs that can help you notice changes in a mole. Not every melanoma shows all these signs, and benign spots can also have some of them.

Learn more from the American Cancer Society ↗
AAsymmetryTwo halves look different.
BBorderEdges are irregular or blurred.
CColorMultiple colors or uneven shade.
DDiameterOften larger than 6 mm, but can be smaller.
EEvolutionChanges in size, shape, color, or symptoms.
ⓘ

Medical disclaimer

SkinCheck AI is an educational prototype. It does not diagnose skin cancer, determine whether a lesion is benign or malignant, or provide medical treatment. A normal-looking image or demo result must not delay medical care. Only a qualified clinician can assess your skin and determine whether further testing, including biopsy, is needed.
